## 1. The claimed accomplishment

CA86 generated an explicit covering array:

    CA(86; 3, 40, 3)

Meaning:

- 86 rows (test configurations).
- 40 columns (variables).
- 3 possible symbols per column.
- Every combination of values for every selection of 3 distinct columns appears in at least one row.

There are 9,880 three-column selections, each requiring 27 combinations, for 266,760 total coverage requirements.

The construction has passed multiple independent exhaustive verifiers, including a separately implemented reviewer checker.

Candidate SHA-256:

    7dc7ab1e231dce3cde5cc1c6b35c4911f34145fd4dfe5d80f8ea26b235b27f8a

The mathematical consequence is:

    CAN(3,40,3) ≤ 86

Here CAN(t,k,v) denotes the minimum number of rows in a covering array of strength t, with k columns and v symbols.

### The previous published record

On November 14, 2026, the maintained `ca-tools` tables reported 87 rows as the best-known upper bound for these parameters.

A prior search checked 110 applicable frame entries and 113 provenance entries, including wider arrays that imply constructions on 40 columns. None established 86 or fewer rows.

The new construction improves that published upper bound from 87 to 86.

The construction was not submitted or publicly published when the handoff was produced.

**Your task is to challenge the novelty claim, not to accept the previous search's conclusion.**
